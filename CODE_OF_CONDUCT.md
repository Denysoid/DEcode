# DEcode Code of Conduct

[Русская версия](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

DEcode should be a place where people can report a problem, question a design, and contribute code without being dismissed or attacked. Technical disagreement is expected. Personal hostility is not.

## Our standard

Good participation includes:

- discussing the work rather than the person;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- admitting mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, and accessibility needs;
- protecting private information found in reports, logs, screenshots, or repositories;
- stepping away from a heated discussion before it becomes personal.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sustained personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, humiliation, deliberate misgendering, or repeated disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring someone to disclose credentials, private code, identity, or contact details;
- using project spaces to promote unrelated spam, scams, or abuse;
- retaliating against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

Criticism of code, documentation, decisions, and project direction is allowed. Keep it relevant, factual, and proportionate.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, releases, and other spaces managed by the DEcode project. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project, for example through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting a problem

If it is safe and useful, ask the person to stop or move the discussion back to the technical issue. You are not required to confront anyone before reporting.

For a private report, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** in the GitHub repository and start the title with `[Conduct]`. The shared private channel is intentional: it prevents sensitive conduct reports from becoming public issues. If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue asking the maintainer for a private contact method, but do not include incident details.

A useful report contains:

- links or dates that identify what happened;
- a concise description of the behavior;
- relevant context and participants;
- screenshots or quotations when they can be shared safely;
- any immediate safety or privacy concern.

Do not include credentials, unrelated private messages, or private repository content. Reports will be shared only with people needed to assess and resolve them. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when disclosure is required for safety, law, or a fair response, but unnecessary publication will be avoided.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock a thread, request a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban an account. The response depends on severity, impact, history, and whether harmful behavior continues after a clear request to stop.

Typical progression:

1. **Correction** — a private or public request to edit language or behavior.
2. **Warning** — a clear statement of the violation and consequences of repetition.
3. **Temporary restriction** — limited participation for a stated period.
4. **Permanent restriction** — removal from project spaces for sustained or severe abuse.

Serious threats, doxxing, targeted harassment, or credible safety risks may skip earlier steps. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and hand a report to another suitable reviewer when possible.

## Appeals

An affected person may request reconsideration through the same private reporting channel. State what decision should change and provide any missing context. Repeating the original argument without new information does not require a new outcome.
